# Viewly design direction

Reading this as: a Windows desktop player for classroom film viewing, using VLC's classic media-player layout, dials ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

The owner asked Viewly to look very similar to VLC and confirmed that as an intentional exception to antislop R-30.

- Palette: light Windows surfaces, a black video stage, and VLC orange for playback actions keep controls familiar while video stays the focal point.
- Layout: a conventional menu, searchable media list, large playback area, and compact transport controls follow the familiar desktop-player order.
- Typography: Segoe UI follows native Windows conventions and stays readable on school displays.
- Spacing and shape: tight spacing, thin separators, and small corner radii echo a practical desktop media tool instead of floating cards.
- Interaction: focus borders and native keyboard navigation make the compact controls usable without a mouse.
- Motion: no decorative animation; motion comes from the video itself, so playback remains the only moving content.
- Identity: use the name Viewly as text and do not reproduce VLC's logo or application icon.
